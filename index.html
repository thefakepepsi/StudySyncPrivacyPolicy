1. Who we are and scope of this policy

StudySync is a tutoring and education platform developed by EduPulse ("EduPulse", "we", "us", "our"), founded by Eashan Falnikar. StudySync includes the following features referred to throughout this policy:

  • Main AI Terminal — the primary AI-assisted tutoring interface;
  • Ledger — account, subscription and usage records;
  • Career Counselor ("Waypoint") — a multilingual AI career-guidance tool;
  • The VR Hub — VR learning experiences across Chemistry, Biology, Geography and Physics, usable with EduPulse-compatible headsets.

This policy applies to StudySync's website, sign-in flow and connected services, wherever they are accessed. It is written to meet our obligations to users in the United Arab Emirates under the PDPL and to users in India under the DPDP Act, because StudySync serves schools, NGOs and individual learners in both countries and offers Hindi, Urdu and Tamil among its supported languages. If a stricter local law applies to a particular user, that stricter standard governs for that user.

StudySync serves schools, NGOs and individual students, including children under 18. Where a school or NGO enrolls students on our platform, that institution acts as an intermediary and is responsible for obtaining any consents required from parents/guardians before providing student data to us, in addition to the safeguards described in Section 7.

3. How we collect data

  • Directly from you — when you sign in with Google, complete a profile, use the AI Terminal, VR Hub or Waypoint, or contact support.
  • From a school or NGO — where an institution enrolls a class or cohort, it may provide student names, grades and contact details to set up accounts.
  • Automatically — through cookies, session logs and region detection used to set your default language and theme.

4. Why we use your data

  • To create and maintain your StudySync account and authenticate sign-in via Google;
  • To provide AI-assisted tutoring, VR learning modules and career-guidance conversations;
  • To personalize language, theme and content to your region and preferences;
  • To manage subscriptions, pricing tiers and billing through Ledger;
  • To monitor for misuse of AI tools in ways inconsistent with academic integrity, consistent with EduPulse's mission to curb AI misuse in schools;
  • To maintain platform security, prevent fraud and troubleshoot technical issues;
  • To communicate service updates, and, where you have agreed, other communications;
  • To comply with legal obligations in the UAE and India.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use children's data to build advertising profiles or for behavioral advertising or targeted marketing.

5. Our legal basis for processing

Under the UAE PDPL, we process personal data on grounds including: your consent, performance of a contract with you or the enrolling school/NGO, compliance with a legal obligation, and our legitimate interest in operating and securing the platform, balanced against your rights. Under the DPDP Act, we process personal data on the basis of your (or your parent/guardian's) consent, given for specified, lawful purposes, or where a "certain legitimate use" recognized by the Act applies (for example, where you voluntarily provide data to us for a service you are seeking).

6. Sharing, third parties and international transfers

We share personal data only as needed to operate StudySync:

  • Google — for account sign-in (Google Sign-In) and authentication;
  • AI service providers — the Career Counselor (Waypoint) uses Google's Gemini API to generate career-guidance responses; conversation inputs you submit to Waypoint are sent to this third-party processor to produce a response;
  • Payment processors — to process school, NGO and individual subscription payments in AED;
  • The enrolling school or NGO — administrators of an institution's account may access usage and progress data for their own students;
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers — to store and run the platform securely;
  • Regulators or authorities — where required by UAE or Indian law.

We do not sell or rent personal data to third parties for their own marketing purposes. Where data is transferred outside the UAE or India (for example, to a cloud or AI provider's servers abroad), we take steps to ensure the recipient offers an adequate level of protection, consistent with PDPL cross-border transfer requirements and the DPDP Act's rules on transfers outside India, including contractual safeguards with our processors.

7. Children's privacy and parental consent

Because StudySync is used by children in a tutoring context, we apply additional safeguards:

  • Parental/guardian or institutional consent. Where a child accesses StudySync directly rather than through a school or NGO account, we require verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian before processing the child's personal data, in line with DPDP Act requirements for processing children's data and PDPL protections for minors. Where a school or NGO enrolls a child, that institution confirms to us that it has the necessary parental/guardian authorization consistent with its own policies.
  • No behavioral tracking or targeted advertising directed at children. We do not use a child's data for targeted advertising or to build a behavioral profile of the child, and we do not carry out tracking or monitoring of children beyond what is strictly necessary to provide the tutoring service and maintain platform safety.
  • Data minimization. We collect only the data needed to deliver tutoring, VR learning and career guidance to the child — for example, a grade/age band rather than a full date of birth where possible.
  • Parental/guardian rights. A parent or guardian may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their child's data, or withdraw consent for further processing, at any time, using the contact details in Section 15. Withdrawing consent may limit or end the child's access to StudySync features.
  • School and NGO responsibility. Where StudySync is deployed by a school or NGO, that institution is responsible for confirming it has the appropriate legal basis (such as parental consent or another basis permitted by its local education regulator) before submitting student data to us.

8. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in Section 4, including to maintain your account, meet contractual obligations to schools/NGOs, and comply with UAE and Indian legal, tax and regulatory requirements. When an account is closed or a student is unenrolled, we delete or anonymize personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law or to resolve disputes.

9. Security

We apply technical and organizational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data we hold, including access controls, encryption in transit, and restricting access to student and account data to personnel and processors who need it to operate StudySync. No system is completely secure, and we encourage users to safeguard their own account credentials.

10. Cookies and similar technologies

StudySync uses cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your language and theme preference, detect your region for language auto-detection, and understand platform usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling some cookies may affect StudySync's functionality, such as automatic language detection or staying signed in.

11. Your rights — UAE (PDPL)

If you are located in the UAE, or your data is processed in connection with our UAE operations, the PDPL gives you the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is processed;
  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Request correction or completion of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Request erasure of your data in certain circumstances;
  • Restrict or object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interest or for direct marketing;
  • Request data portability, where technically feasible;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal.

12. Your rights — India (DPDP Act)

If you are located in India, or you are a "Data Principal" under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Obtain a summary of the personal data we process about you and the processing activities carried out;
  • Request correction, completion or updating of your personal data;
  • Request erasure of your personal data, unless retention is required for a specified purpose or by law;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as it was given;
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity;
  • Have readily available means to register a grievance with us (see Section 13).

Where a request concerns a child, it may be made by the child's parent or lawful guardian.

13. Grievance redressal and complaints

If you have a concern about how your (or your child's) data has been handled, you can contact our Data Protection / Grievance contact using the details in Section 15. We aim to acknowledge and resolve grievances within a reasonable time frame. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may also raise the matter with the UAE Data Office (the PDPL regulator) or, for users in India, the Data Protection Board of India, as applicable.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or in UAE or Indian law. We will update the "Last updated" date above and, where changes are material — particularly changes affecting children's data — we will take reasonable steps to notify schools, NGOs, parents/guardians or users directly.
